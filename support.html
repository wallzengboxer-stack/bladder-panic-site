Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
When nature calls, time slows down. Compare each route's travel time with "Can hold" at the top-left and pick a toilet. On the way, tap left/right to dodge, swipe up to jump, swipe down to duck, and hold Sprint to run faster (but it makes you need to go more). The full guide is in Settings (gear icon) → Help.

I keep failing a level. Is there one right answer?
No. Routes, hidden risks and surprises change on every attempt, and every attempt can be won. The Binoculars item lets you peek at hidden risks.

What are CLENCH and ABOUT TO BURST?
In tummy levels, tap CLENCH when the ring hits green. At 90% you hit ABOUT TO BURST: mash to hold it back and get a speed boost.

Does it work on iPad and foldable iPhone?
Yes. The game supports portrait and landscape, iPad Split View and Stage Manager, and iPhone Duo — fold or unfold at any time and the layout adjusts.

Do I need the internet or an account?
No. It works fully offline. Game Center is optional.

How do I change the language?
Tap the gear on the title screen → Language. 14 languages are supported.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
